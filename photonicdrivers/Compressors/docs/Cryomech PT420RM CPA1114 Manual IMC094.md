Overview
Section 1: Overview
This section provides a general overview of a cryocooler, the organizational structure of
this manual, and a glossary of defined terms and acronyms.
1.1 Cryocooler system
1.1.1 General description
The operation of a Cryomech cryocooler is based on a closed-loop helium expansion cycle.
The cryocooler consists of a helium compressor package, cold head, flex lines and cold
head motor cord.
The compressor package receives low pressure helium from the cold head, pressurizes
the helium gas and returns high pressure helium gas to the cold head via flex lines. The
cold head reaches cryogenic temperatures through adiabatic expansion by using the high
pressure helium gas from the helium compressor package and discharging low pressure
helium gas and returning it to the compressor package.
1.1.2 Organization of the manual
This manual is divided into eight section that provide the information needed to install,
operate and perform maintenance on a Cryomech cryocooler. It is important to note that
bulleted list may be performed in any order but numbered lists (labeled 1, 2, etc.) are
representative of sequential actions that must be performed in the order listed.
1.2 Glossary
1.2.1 Definitions
Aeroquip Couplings:
The term "Aeroquip couplings" is used generically to describe the self-sealing fittings that
connect components, e.g. helium flex lines are connected to the helium compressor
package and to the cold head via Aeroquip couplings.
Category II Installation:
Category II refers to the potential for transient over-voltage conditions in the mains power
connection to the equipment. See IEC 664, Sub-clause 5.6 for further details.
Closed Loop System:
This refers to a cryogenic system that has no helium loss.
Cold Head:
The cold head is an expansion device which is capable of reaching cryogenic
temperatures.
Cold Head Motor Cord:
The cold head motor cord provides electrical power from the helium compressor package
to the cold head motor.
1-1

Overview
Cold Head Heat Exchanger:
The heat exchanger(s) on the cold head provide cooling at cryogenic temperatures by
transferring heat to the helium within the system.
Cryogenic Temperatures:
Temperatures lower than 120 K or -153 °C.
Cryocooler:
A cryocooler is a cryogenic refrigeration system based upon a closed loop helium
expansion cycle. It consists of a helium compressor package, helium flex lines, and a cold
head (expansion device).
Gifford-McMahon (GM):
A cold head which expands the helium using a displacer.
Helium Compressor Package:
Provides system safety controls, houses the components that compress, cool and purify
the helium gas and provides electrical interface controls.
Helium Flex Lines:
The helium flex lines are corrugated, stainless steel hoses that transport helium between
the helium compressor package and the cold head.
Pollution Degree 2:
Pollution degree 2 refers to the extent to which the local environmental conditions could
affect the electrical safety of the system. See EN 61010 or UL 61010A for further details.
Pulse Tube:
A cold head that does not use a displacer or piston.
System:
The term "system" is used as a synonym for cryocooler. It consists of a compressor
package, helium flex lines and cold head.

4. Locate and remove items that can easily be lifted out of the crate.
5. Remove the sides of the packing crate by unfastening the Klimp fasteners and
screws that join the sides to the pallet.

the packing materials and crate for use in the future, to ship the equipment to

5.2.5. Safety Devices
Several safety devices are located inside the helium compressor package and on the cold head.
They are monitored by the helium compressor package’s control panel to maintain a safe working
environment and protect the helium compressor package and cold head from developing extreme
conditions that can cause damage, see Section 7 for further detail. The safety devices listed below
are not monitored by the helium compressor package’s control panel.
High-Pressure Atmospheric Relief Valve
The helium compressor package high-pressure atmospheric relief valve is set at
420 ± 5 psig (29 ± .34 bar). At pressures above 420 psig (29 bar) the relief valve will open
automatically and relieve pressure to the atmosphere.
Internal Motor Overload Switch
An internal motor overload switch, located inside the helium compressor package module, protects
the system by sensing excessive current draw and temperature. This switch automatically resets

The digital I/O DB15 female connector is described in this section. Enabling the inputs require a

Inputs
SLVL (Pin 12)
When SLVL is enabled, the Start pin must remain enabled for the system to run. Enabling the Start

the system will enter Stopping mode. If the Compressor OFF Button, Screen OFF Button, Modbus
or SMDP OFF command, Remote Interlock pin or Stop pin are enabled, while the system is running
the system will enter Stopping mode. If the system is off when the Remote Interlock pin or Stop pin

start commands. If the system is running when the Remote Interlock is enabled, the system will
enter Stopping mode.
Stop (Pin 14)
When the Stop is enabled, the system cannot be started. Activating Stop disables the Compressor
ON Button, Screen ON Button, System I/O Start, Modbus and SMDP start commands. If the system
is running when Stop is enabled, the system will enter Stopping mode.
